# antibiotics for treatment of community acquired pneumonia

- related: [[community acquired pneumonia]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

## Outpatient

for outpatient treatment, it depends on if patient has comorbidity or not.

- no comorbidity and young:
	- amoxicillin: 1 g TID
	- doxycycline: 100 BID
	- azithromycin: 500, then 250 daily
- comorbidity or old:
	- augmentin 800 BID and macrolide/doxycycline.
	- fluoroquinolone as alternative: levofloxacin 750 mg daily or moxi at 400 daily

Treatment generally should be around 5 days [^1]
