## Delirium Pharm Treatment

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027131230.png)

- works, doesn't work, no difference
- Melatonin should be ==dosed 1.5 to 2 hours== before desired sleep time

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027131402.png)

- low dose can help with delirium resolution, fewer delirium active days

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027131456.png)

- new drug, smoother sleep effect, less side effects
- can work for insomnia
- don't continue Ambien/Lunesta inpatient => can increase delirium risks
- needs to fail melatonin, trazodone, ambien before approval

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027131730.png)

- increased mortality
- maybe short term benefit
- no longterm benefit
- stop when leaving icu

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027133400.png)

- higher cycle sensitivity = breath cut off sooner
- e.g. 60% of peak flow is sooner than 20% of peak flow
- Ti min: 0.6
- Ti long: 1.5

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027133641.png)

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261027133722.png)

- higher trigger sensitivity: sometimes autopeep can trigger a breath => keep trigger in middle
- COPD: shorten rise time can feel they can get the breath quicker
