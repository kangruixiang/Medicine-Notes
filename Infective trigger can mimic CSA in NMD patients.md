# Infective trigger can mimic CSA in NMD patients

- related: [[Sleep and Sleep Disordered Breathing]]
- tags: #literature #sleep
---

Infecctive trigger in spontaneous mode in NMD can look very similar to central sleep apnea with high AHI. You can avoid this by either increase trigger sensitivity ([[NIV initial settings]]) or switch to BPAP-ST mode or home mechanical ventilators ([[respiratory assist devices vs home mechanical ventilators]], [[AirCurve 10 different modes available]]).
