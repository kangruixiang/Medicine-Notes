# ECMO when to initiate or refer

- related: [[ECMO extracorporeal membrane oxygenation]]
- tags: #literature #icu
---
- initiate VV ecmo when P:F < 80, refractory hypoxemia or hypercapnia with pCO2 > 60
	- Use RESP score
	- bronchopleural fistula, ARDS, obstruction
- Use Va ecmo for refractory cardiogenic shock or cardiac arrest
	- PE, overdose
	- use SAVE score
