## ILD

- **Scleroderma Lung Study I:** cyclophosphamide compared with placebo was associated with a modest improvement in FVC
- **Scleroderma Lung Study II**: mycophenolate (cellcept) works as well as cyclophosphamide (cytoxan) in scleroderma-ILD [^1]
- **SENSCIS**: nintedanib slowed the decline in FVC as compared with placebo over 12 months, with half the patients in each arm on background mycophenolate mofetil therapy [^2]

[^1]: [[Mycophenolate mofetil versus oral cyclophosphamide in scleroderma-related interstitial lung disease (SLS II) - a randomised controlled, double-blind, parallel group trial]]
[^2]: [[Nintedanib for Systemic Sclerosis–Associated Interstitial Lung Disease]]
