## Highlights

- Category C [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=YZIJ4T6P)
- PESI >85 [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=HNVXTL6F)
- sPESI ≥1 [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=ACVPNS9N)
- The respiratory modifier, R, is applied when hypoxemia or tachypnea [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=ESSSRL67)
- need for supplemental oxygen [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=HJ3AWZVK)
- Category D [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=Z44E7ESH)
- normotensive shock or approaching need for ventilatory support [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=7KCZ7YNT)
- Category D1 identifies patients with transient or recurrent hypotension [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=WLW59Z9W)
- short-lived [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=X4578UZY)
- responds to volume expansion [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=69RZFLJY)
- Category D2 requires a marker of decreased perfusion or end-organ dysfunction [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=Q365EA3W)
- respiratory modifier, R, would be applied if the patient required either >6 L [p.e991](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e991&annotation=UVT4U2IM)
- use of LMWH reduces recurrent VTE risk more effectively than UFH without increasing major bleeding risk in patients with acute PE [p.e1003](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1003&annotation=6GLSGMHS)
- In patients who have undergone bariatric surgery, however, DOACs should be avoided for at least 4 weeks after their procedure [p.e1003](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1003&annotation=7SHGFGMB)
- There is 1 small RCT evaluating a lower versus a standard dose of enoxaparin (0.8 mg/kg versus 1.0 mg/kg actual body weight twice daily) in patients with severe obesity (BMI ≥40 kg/m2) [p.e1004](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1004&annotation=YHJAS9PT)
- Pregnant patients with acute PE [p.e1005](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1005&annotation=RZIVP7YX)
- LMWH is used more commonly than UFH in pregnant patients [p.e1005](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1005&annotation=RDPG85LI)
- Therefore, the use of DOACs for patients with acute PE and severe [p.e1005](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1005&annotation=7RQNUDBV)
- hepatic impairment may result in harm and should be avoided. [p.e1005](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1005&annotation=YHAWNFP2)
- norepinephrine (NE) is generally considered the vasopressor of choice [p.e1007](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1007&annotation=LVAHILTH)
- At doses ≤15 μg/min, NE has little to no effect on PVR [p.e1007](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1007&annotation=BBM726MG)
- However, at doses exceeding 15 μg/min, NE may increase PVR [p.e1007](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1007&annotation=5ML2PBTK)
- continuous infusion of dobutamine (up to 10 μg/ kg/min) [p.e1007](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1007&annotation=NRAVTFHP)
- Thus, any amount of sedation should be administered with caution when there is evidence or concern for RV dysfunction. [p.e1008](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1008&annotation=PDJGKQPV)
- deeper sedation and intubation should be avoided [p.e1008](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1008&annotation=YIYBJMZI)
- In patients with acute, refractory cardiogenic shock as a result of known or suspected acute PE (AHA/ ACC PE Category E2), it is reasonable to institute VA-ECMO [p.e1008](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1008&annotation=CTAXIVE2)
- IVC filters can be useful to reduce the short-term incidence of recurrent PE. [p.e1009](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1009&annotation=4YFS47AP)
- The PREPIC trial demonstrated that IVC filters led to significantly higher rates of DVT and IVC thrombosis over time without improving long-term survival [p.e1009](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1009&annotation=G4VJVFTY)
- PREPIC2 trial [p.e1009](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1009&annotation=QGPS8SXD)
- found no added benefit in preventing recurrent PE [p.e1009](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1009&annotation=QTT9S8GD)
- IVC filter retrieval within 29 and 54 days [p.e1010](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1010&annotation=EX6EAENR)
- who have evidence of freefloating right atrial and/or RV clot-in-transit, the utilization of advanced therapies over anticoagulation alone is reasonable [p.e1011](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1011&annotation=RZC9BSK8)
- Categories E1-2 [p.e1012](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1012&annotation=BQ93ZHTC)
- systemic thrombolysis and anticoagulation is reasonable over anticoagulation alone [p.e1012](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1012&annotation=H68E7KLV)
- AHA/ACC PE Categories D1-2 [p.e1012](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1012&annotation=9ANV2JNL)
- in whom advanced therapy is being considered, systemic thrombolysis and anticoagulation [p.e1012](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1012&annotation=DUZU7E5S)
- acute PE in AHA/ACC PE Categories A1-C2, systemic thrombolysis should not be used over anticoagulation alone [p.e1012](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1012&annotation=8CWG8P9F)
- Category E1, CDL plus anticoagulation [p.e1013](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1013&annotation=KL8MCHFB)
- acute PE in AHA/ACC PE Category E1, it is reasonable to choose MT plus anticoagulation over anticoagulation alone [p.e1015](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1015&annotation=S7YICSF7)
- In patients with acute PE in AHA/ACC PE Category E1, surgical embolectomy compared with anticoagulation alone is reasonable [p.e1016](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LXRI3FSS?page=e1016&annotation=INNJYFMM)
