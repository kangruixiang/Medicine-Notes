## Persistent Notes

%% begin notes %%

- for outpatient treatment, it depends on if patient has comorbidity or not.
	- no comorbidity and young: amoxicillin, doxycycline, azithromycin
	- comorbidity or old: augmentin and macrolide/doxycycline. Can use fluoroquinolone as alternative

## Highlights

- Severe CAP [p.634](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=634&annotation=NLWTGEQK)
- Septic shock [p.634](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=634&annotation=ASILJHKP)
- Respiratory failure [p.634](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=634&annotation=5WNMTXDD)
- amoxicillin (1 g three times daily), doxycycline (100 mg twice daily), or a macrolide (azithromycin at a dose of 500 mg on day 1, then 250 mg daily, or clarithromycin at a dose of 500 mg twice daily [extendedrelease, 1000 mg daily]). [p.636](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=636&annotation=34DCIA4K)
- amoxicillin–clavulanate (875 mg orally twice daily [extended-release, 2 g twice daily]) and either a macrolide (preferred) or doxycycline are recommended. [p.636](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=636&annotation=AHXBABV9)
- fluoroquinolone (levofloxacin at a dose of 750 mg daily or moxifloxacin at a dose of 400 mg daily) or one of two more recently approved agents, lefamulin or omadacycline [p.636](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=636&annotation=Q9DIYTV6)
- Treatment should usually continue for a minimum of 5 days; [p.639](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=639&annotation=9AVTILTH)
- Extended courses of therapy may be indicated for patients with immunocompromising conditions, infections caused by certain pathogens (e.g., P. aeruginosa), or complications such as empyema. [p.639](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BYKG937J?page=639&annotation=2SSI2U6V)
