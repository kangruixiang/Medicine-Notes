## First 48 Hours

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133047.png)

- majority of mortality in first 30 days

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133208.png)

- low flow may be evidence of tamponade

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133329.png)

- plan for any RV dysfunction

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133505.png)

- camera should be at apex of LV, pointed toward MV, no contact with septum

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133556.png)

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133616.png)

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133820.png)

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903133900.png)

- decrease preload, decreased RV size, PCWP: think about bleed or tamponade
- maintain lower MAP

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260903134127.png)

- maintain lower MAP first, avoid high MAP
- [[PAPi is ratio of pulmonary artery pulse pressure to right atrial pressure]]
