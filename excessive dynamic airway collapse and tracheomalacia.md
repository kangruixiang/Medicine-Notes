# excessive dynamic airway collapse and tracheomalacia

- related: [[Pulmonary Diseases]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

## Overview

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260802144628.png)

- tracheomalacia and EDAC are both part of excessive central airway collapse
- tracheomalacia is ==widening== of coronal dimension and ==narrowing== of sagittal dimension from ==weakening of the cartilages==
- EDAC is ==anterior bowing== of posterior membrane from ==weakening of longitudinal elastic fibers==, cartilage is ok
- severity:
	- mild: 70%
	- moderate 80-90%
	- severe: over 90%

## Diagnosis

- you can see it on CT scan
- PFT can often show variable expiratory obstruction![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20260802145614.png)

## Treatment

- tracheobronchomalacia are often symptomatic and need treatment: e.g. tracheobronchoplasty (TBP)
- EDAC may or may not be symptomatic. [^1]
