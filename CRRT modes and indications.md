# CRRT modes and indications

- related: [[CRRT continuous renal replacement therapy]]
- tags: #literature #nephrology
---

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131253.png)

- CVVH: depends on pressure between two sides, flow of fluid, pore size of membrane, then drag of solute with it
- CVVHD: diffusive. Gradient between two sides
- HDF: both solvent drag and diffusion.

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131358.png)

- only for fluid removal
- dependent on pressures
- A pump creates negative pressure
- doesn't really do it here

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131440.png)

- substitution: replacement fluid
- pump creates negative pressure
- solute and fluid both dragged across membrane

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131652.png)

- counter current dialysate going opposite direction as blood flow. Has gradient and diffusion
- good for smaller molecules
- can add therapy fluid to create CVVHDF

![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131701.png)![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131729.png)![](https://photos.thisispiggy.com/file/wikiFiles/20261105131904.png)

- uremia: CVVHD
- fluid + solute: CVVH
- fluid: SCUF
