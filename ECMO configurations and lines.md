## VV

There are generally three types of configuration:

- femoral-femoral: also familiar with decreased mobility. Also has recirculation problem.
- femoral-IJ: most familiar, easiest. Decreased mobility
- 1 cannula IJ: typically avoided but best for mobility and proning[^1]
