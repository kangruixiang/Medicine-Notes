# use of brensocatib reduces exacerbation in bronchiectasis

- related: [[chronic bronchiectasis]]
- tags: #permanent #pulmonology
---

- bronchiectasis have higher level of neutrophils with elevated IL-8
- WILLLOW trial showed reduced exacerbation in 24 week period, lower level of neutrophil inflammation and elastase[^1]
- ASPEN trial: use of rensocatib lowers annual exacerbation rate and decline of FEV1[^2]
