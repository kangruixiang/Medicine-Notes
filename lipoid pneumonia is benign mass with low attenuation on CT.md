# lipoid pneumonia is benign mass with low attenuation on CT

- related: [[Pulmonary Diseases]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

Images show nodular opacity containing low attenuation, which measures ==fat attenuation==. Thus, the nodular opacity represents ==lipoid pneumonia==. Lipoid pneumonia is caused by ==aspiration of mineral oil==. The finding does not require additional workup.

- biopsy with unspecific pathology ("MINUTE FRAGMENT OF FIBROUS TISSUE WITH CHRONIC INFLAMMATION.")
