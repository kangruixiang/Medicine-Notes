# When to suspect pulmonary hypertension

- related: [[pulmonary hypertension]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

## Physical signs

- Precordial heave due to RV hypertrophy
- Loud P2, right sided S3 and or S4
- Fixed splitting S2
- Holosystolic murmur from tricuspid regirgutation[^1]

## PFT

- [[COPD and pulmonary hypertension COPD-PH]]
	- look for severe dyspnea with mild emphysema
	- FEV1/DLCO ratio > 1.4
- [[ILD and pulmonary hypertension ILD-PH]]
	- FVC/DLCO% > 1.6
	- Use FORD score for predicting
	- CorVista POC test
- severe desaturation on walk test with HR recovery in 1 min

## Imaging

- PA/Ao >0.93

## Labs

- ProBNP > 650

## EKG

- PH-EDA for EKG prediction

[^1]: [[SEEK Questionnaires]]
