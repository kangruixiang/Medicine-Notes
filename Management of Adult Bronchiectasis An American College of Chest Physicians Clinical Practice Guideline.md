## Persistent Notes

%% begin notes %%

- no evidence for shorter vs longer antibiotics duration
- no recommendation for eradication of first culture positive for pseudomonas
- use inhaled antibiotic for frequent ≥ 2 exacerbation
	- tobramycin, ciprofloxacin, colistin, gentamicin
- use azithromycin: decreases exacerbation by 1, improves symptom but not statistically significant
- use brensocatib: decreases exacerbation by 1, improves FEV1/FVC, QoL. Can use with azithromycin
- consider surgery for local bronchiectasis
