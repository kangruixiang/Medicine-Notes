- related: [[COPD chronic obstructive pulmonary disease]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

- ==PVR > 5== in COPD patients is very bad and has poor outcome
- screen for ==pulmonary vascular phenotype== (PVP): these are patients with mild COPD but seemingly very short of breath and has hypoxemia.
