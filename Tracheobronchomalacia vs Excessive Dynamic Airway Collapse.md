## Highlights

- intrathoracic, extratracheal pressure is greater than the intrathoracic intratracheal pressure [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=P925UDWX)
- approximately 30% physiologic reduction [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=RYXIS5HX)
- It consists of 2 distinct entities: tracheobronchomalacia (TBM) and excessive dynamic airway collapse [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=D98GPPEQ)
- widening of the coronal tracheobronchial dimension [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=Y8H6PZIE)
- concomitant narrowing of the sagittal tracheobronchial dimension [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=IHBZ2HS4)
- anterior bowing of the posterior membranous wall [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=JU6GDKR4)
- without deformation of the cartilaginous structure [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=4YNNCC44)
- moderate when the cross-sectional area reduction is 80% to 89% [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=HKEYQ8GC)
- severe when the cross-sectional area reduction is greater than or equal to 90% [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=QK27YXGB)
- Tracheobronchomalacia (TBM) is a structural airway abnormality [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=LTSKF47B)
- cartilaginous weakening of the trachea and bronchi [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=UXHDJ7TT)
- Excessive dynamic airway collapse, on the other hand, is characterized by abnormal anterior bowing of the posterior membrane with a normal tracheal cartilage [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=55LID8UJ)
- Awake dynamic bronchoscopy is the gold standard [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=9TLL7VH7)
- greater than or equal to70% [p.123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=123&annotation=67AWTMAG)
- dynamic computed tomography (CT) scan of the chest with expiratory and inspiratory phases and awake bronchoscopy [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=XFVRH4X4)
- When the geometry of the tracheobronchial cartilage is preserved throughout respiration [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=S6KSPCQT)
- EDAC [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=9ENKTFBP)
- when the airway is noted to be wider in the coronal dimension and narrower in the sagittal dimension [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=A8HMSQAC)
- normal C-shaped geometry of the tracheal bronchial cartilage is no longer visualized [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=K39SCIGN)
- TBM [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=ZPNGH2QH)
- 14.1% to 23.4% in patients with chronic cough [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=7CPIICHS)
- up to 53% in patients with stable COPD [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=GMESUYXE)
- causing atrophy of the tracheal or bronchial cartilages and fibers of the membranous portions [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=XKLZB83B)
- airway stabilization and stenting [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=RR55ATLS)
- bronchoscopic stenting versus routine bronchoscopic stenting [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=4EEDMZKV)
- tracheobronchoplasty [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=R9JPNUXU)
- Flow-volume loops from PFTs can demonstrate fixed as well as variable airway obstruction [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=WQIPTSVY)
- Variable airway obstruction can be extrathoracic or intrathoracic [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=58U25JA6)
- Both EDAC and TBM [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=G4E88UXT)
- as variable [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=NW4LWF5N)
- TBM limits airflow and causes symptoms [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=R82ASW4N)
- debate exists as to whether EDAC results in air flow limitation [p.124](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=124&annotation=GLFYNWZS)
- seal-like barky cough [p.125](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/KARNSI3L?page=125&annotation=9XPIPWZI)
