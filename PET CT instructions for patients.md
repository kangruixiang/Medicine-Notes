# PET CT instructions for patients

- related: [[chest imaging]]
- tags: #literature #pulmonology
---

**Standard preparation**:
You may have a high protein, low carbohydrate diet 24 hours prior to your exam.
Do not eat or drink 6 hours prior.
Please do not do any strenuous exercise for 24 hours prior.
Please wear non-metal containing clothing. If clothes contain metal, you will be asked to remove clothing items prior to scan.
If you plan to take an oral sedative prescribed by your provider, you will need to bring a driver.
If you have a port and prefer that it be used for your exam, please ensure it is accessed prior to your appointment.
 
**Diabetic Patients**:
Do not eat or drink 6 hours prior to exam.
Oral medication may be taken with water.
Fasting blood sugar must be at or below 250 mg/dl.
Do not take insulin during the 6 hour fasting period.
